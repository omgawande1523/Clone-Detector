How SwarKavach protects a phone

The same three-check design as our enterprise server, moved onto the phone. Every model runs on the device; audio is analysed in 5-second windows in memory and thrown away. Only scores, reasons and a hashed number are stored.

1PolicyUnknown numbers always checked. Contacts set to Ignore are skipped. "Protect" contacts get their voice verified.
2CaptureLive (accessibility + mic on speaker), after-call recording scan, or built-in system access.
3CleanSilero VAD keeps speech; owner voiceprint removes your own side of the call.
4Three checksSynthetic voice (wav2vec2) · voiceprint match (WeSpeaker) · scam context (rules + offline speech recognition).
5FusionNoisy-OR with caps 0.90 / 0.90 / 0.30: one strong signal can alert; context alone never can.
6Risk engineAsymmetric EMA + two-window rule, so a cough or line glitch can't trigger an alarm.
7ActFull-screen alert, 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in / Chakshu, hash-chained audit record.

Three ways to hear the caller

ModeHowPlay Store
LiveAccessibility service + foreground mic service while the call is on speaker. Starts automatically or with one tap.sideload / enterprise
After-callScans the dialer's own recording seconds after hang-up and warns before you act on the call.allowed
Built-inPre-installed by a phone maker or operator with system call-audio permission (the production path).allowed

Android does not give ordinary apps call audio since Android 10, so we designed around it instead of pretending otherwise.

Known limits (we say them first)

  • Some modern commercial AI voices slip past the acoustic model (a commercial TTS sample scored 0.004). The voiceprint check still applies to Protect contacts.
  • One real-person phone recording was falsely flagged (0.995). Narrowband 8 kHz audio flagged 4 of 48 genuine clips. Retraining on phone-codec and Indian speech is first on the roadmap.
  • Hindi speech recognition costs 3 s per window on this phone, over our real-time budget; production would use a lighter keyword-spotting model.
  • Live capture needs the sideloaded build and speakerphone; store builds use the after-call scan.

Two editions, one engine

  • Phone edition (SwarKavach): Android app, all models on-device, Hindi + English UI.
  • Enterprise edition: FastAPI server + live dashboard for bank and government call centres, plugged into the existing recorded-call stream. Same fusion, same risk engine, 212 automated tests.
  • The phone port reproduces the Python reference exactly: 300/300 fusion, 60/60 risk-engine and 80/80 context golden test cases.
